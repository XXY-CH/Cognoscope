## Remediation record

Implemented the review loop with the following minimal changes:

- **HIGH-1 — `EpubRenderer.tsx`:** keep the `relocated` listener registered
  before both the initial `rendition.display()` and pending locator replay;
  commit `start.location` to `currentPage` and `start.href` to
  `currentEpubHref`; defer the bottom-page display effect until `ready`, so
  the initialization/replay skip marker cannot be consumed while loading and
  overwrite a successful locator replay.
- **MEDIUM-1 — `PdfPageAnnotations.tsx`:** centralize range resolution so a
  malformed, out-of-bounds, or quote-mismatched versioned/legacy offset falls
  through to normalized `quotedText` matching before the locator is marked
  unavailable.
- **MEDIUM-2 — `epubToc.ts`, `TocPanel.tsx`, `TocPanel.module.css`:** omit
  empty-href navigation containers from visible entries while preserving
  their numeric depth; synthesize non-visible hierarchy wrappers so the
  existing tokenized nested-list indentation remains meaningful at arbitrary
  depth.
- **LOW-1 — `EpubRenderer.tsx`, `PdfPageAnnotations.tsx`:** bound tooltip
  previews to 60 characters including the ellipsis.
- **Static probe — `scripts/probe-read04.mjs`:** add relocation ordering and
  bottom-page guard assertions, malformed/out-of-bounds/quote-mismatch PDF
  fallback fixtures, and an empty-container EPUB depth fixture.
