# KG-05 Research Quality Rubric

Pass when the graph helps the researcher decide what to inspect next while
preserving uncertainty:

- Relation badges say origin and `关系线索`; edge weight is not shown as a
  confidence or evidence percentage.
- Match method, locator availability, item verification, and row verification
  remain separate visible fields.
- Resolved anchors can return to the original source, but graph relations,
  unresolved locators, missing files, and disputed material cannot become
  citation-ready through this surface.
- Counterexamples and review states remain visible with an explicit reason.
- A graph selection does not alter authored stance, inferred signal, or matrix
  verification state.

Adversarial checks: graph-only relation, unresolved source, deleted source,
disputed row, and keyword selection spanning multiple papers.
