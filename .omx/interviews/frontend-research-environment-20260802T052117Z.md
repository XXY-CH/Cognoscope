## Condensed transcript

1. The desired scenario is local paper import/download context, personal reading
   and annotation, followed by AI organization, intelligent graphing,
   literature synthesis, research-bias and research-gap analysis.
2. The primary artifacts are a layered personal research graph and cross-paper
   literature review/evidence matrix because they preserve citation and logic
   chains.
3. Both explicit personal positions/preferences and system-inferred interests/
   blind spots should be represented.
4. When explicit stance and inferred preference conflict, create a
   “偏向冲突 / 待审视” research lead rather than overwriting either one.
5. Generate that lead in a post-reading summary so the reading surface remains
   quiet and uninterrupted.
6. Directly save unambiguous source/annotation-derived content and cross-paper
   consensus; keep source/annotation conflicts and inter-paper conflicts as
   reviewable drafts.
7. Cross-paper consensus requires at least three papers, counterexample checks,
   method checks, and dataset-independence checks. Any counterexample downgrades
   the result to “待审核 / 有争议”.
8. This phase should complete local import → reading → AI organization → graph/
   matrix. External paper search/download and TeX/target-journal checks are
   later phases.
9. Returning users should land on current research state, not the raw library.

## Pressure-pass findings

- Personalization was challenged as a possible bias amplifier. The resolution
  is to keep authored stance and inferred signal separate, and turn conflicts
  into review leads rather than hidden verdicts.
- “Consensus” was challenged as a possible false-positive. The resolution is a
  three-paper minimum plus counterexample, method, and dataset-independence
  checks, with any counterexample forcing review.
- Reading interruption was challenged. The resolution is a post-session
  summary, not live analysis popups inside the reader.
